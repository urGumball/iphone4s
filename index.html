Why the iPhone 4s is The Best Old apple device

A timeless design, memorable features, and a surprisingly strong balance of style and functionality.

Why it's the best old apple device:

  • Steve Jobs legacy

    It was the last iPhone that Steve Jobs had his blood in, making it a special moment in Apple history.

  • Siri, for better or worse

    It was the first iPhone with Siri, the early voice assistant that started a new era.

  • iOS 6 and 7 era

    It supports two of the most distinct versions of iOS, giving it a unique place in Apple history.

  • Classic design details

    It has a classic home button and a sleek flat glass design that feels iconic.

  • Retina display

    It was the first iPhone with a Retina display, which was a huge leap forward in screen clarity.

  • Beautiful volume buttons

    Its circular volume buttons are a small detail that made the whole device feel more premium.

  • Front-facing camera

    It was the first iPhone with a front-facing camera, which changed how people used smartphones.

  • Balanced performance

    It delivers a great balance of features and performance, making it easy to love.
